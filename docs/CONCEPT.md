# Robot Cloud System

**Robots as a Service: Your On-Demand Robotic Workforce.**

Robot Cloud System provides on-demand robotic infrastructure. Our platform allows any business or person to leverage a network of autonomous drones and robots, powered by our advanced software, to automate physical tasks and gather data.

## The Problem

Deploying and managing a fleet of robots is complex and expensive. Businesses lack the infrastructure and software to integrate autonomous systems into their daily operations, limiting their ability to automate physical tasks and gather critical data.

Robotics companies all need to deploy their own hardware, search their own permits and licenses, and build their own foundation software. This creates a fragmented and inefficient market, where each company has to reinvent the wheel.

## One Platform, Endless Applications

Our solution is a single, unified platform that leverages a shared network of autonomous drones and robots. Think of it as an "App Store" for robotic services. We deploy the hardware across a community, like Sollentuna, Stockholm or Berlin. The user simply chooses the applications they need and deploys them using the available robots in the network. 

This shared-use model makes advanced robotic solutions accessible and affordable for everyone, from local police and construction companies to home owners wanting to inspect their roofs. We take care of the infrastructure, licensing, backend software, and maintenance.

Developers build applications on top of our platform, creating a vibrant ecosystem of robotic solutions that can be used across various industries without the need for extensive legal, hardware expertise or investment in hardware from the developer side.

Users can easily access and deploy these applications through our platform, enabling them to leverage the power of robotics without the complexity of managing hardware or software.

## How It Works: Tasking a Robot

Our platform makes it simple to deploy robots for any task.


### User Workflow
Using robots is as easy as ordering a ride on a ride-sharing app. Users can deploy robots for various tasks, such as security patrols, infrastructure inspections, or emergency response missions.

1. **Choose the Application**: Select from a range of applications in our app store, such as security patrols, infrastructure inspections, 3D mapping, and more.
2. **Define the Mission**: Set up the mission using the application.
3. **Schedule the Mission**: The mission can be scheduled through our app for immediate, future, or recurring execution.
4.  **Autonomous Execution**: The robot autonomously navigates to the location and executes the mission, collecting data through its sensors.
5. **Data & Control**: All data is logged, and application specific data is analyzed and summarized for you. 
6. **Payment**: You pay for the service based on the application used and the duration of the mission.


### Developer Workflow
Building a hardware enabled SaaS without the need to manage legislation, hardware, or infrastructure is easy with our platform.

1. **Build the Application**: Use our SDK to create applications that can run on our platform.
2. **Integrate with the Platform**: Connect your application to our API for data access and control.
3. **Publish to the App Store**: Submit your application for review and listing in our app store.
4. **Monetize**: Set your pricing model (subscription, pay-per-use, etc.) and earn revenue from users who deploy your application.
5. **Engage with Users**: Monitor usage, gather feedback, and update your application to improve functionality and user experience.
6. **Community Support**: Engage with our developer community for support, collaboration, and sharing best practices.
7. **Expand Your Reach**: As our platform grows, your application can reach a wider audience without the need for additional marketing or sales efforts.


### Hardware Owner Workflow
If you own hardware, you can run our platform on your own robots and drones, allowing you to earn revenue from your assets.

1. **Purchase Hardware**: Acquire compatible drones and robots that can run our software.
2. **Install Our Software**: Deploy our platform software on your hardware to enable remote operation
3. **Connect to the Network**: Register your hardware with our platform to make it available for missions.
4. **Earn Revenue**: Participate in our revenue-sharing model by allowing users to deploy applications
5. **Manage Your Fleet**: Use our platform to monitor and maintain your hardware, ensuring optimal performance and uptime.

## Allocation and Scheduling of Robots
Customers can allocate robots for their specific tasks for long time periods to guarantee availability for a higher premium. This might be beneficial for emergency services, security companies, or critical infrastructure monitoring.

For more cost sensitive tasks, customers can schedule missions using the app store. The system will automatically allocate the best available robot for the task based on location, availability, and required capabilities similar to how spot pricing works in the cloud computing industry.
