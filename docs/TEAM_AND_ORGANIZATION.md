# Team & Organization: Robot Cloud System

This document outlines the organizational structure, key personnel, and talent strategy for the Robot Cloud System. It details current team capabilities, future hiring plans, and organizational governance.

---

## Founding Team (3 Engineering Co-Founders)

### CEO & Co-Founder: Technical Leader with Business Acumen
**Recommended Profile:**
- Strongest in business strategy and market understanding
- Robotics engineering background with commercial experience
- Natural leadership and communication skills for investor/customer relations

**Core Responsibilities:**
- Overall strategy, vision, and company culture
- Investor relations and fundraising (critical for €12M raise)
- Key enterprise customer relationships and sales strategy
- Regulatory affairs and government relations (EASA compliance)
- Public speaking and company representation

**Technical Involvement:** 20% hands-on, 80% strategic oversight

### CTO & Co-Founder: Deep Technical Architecture Leader
**Recommended Profile:**
- Strongest technical depth in autonomous systems and AI/ML
- Experience with Ruby on Rails at scale and API design
- Background in safety-critical systems (aviation, automotive, or industrial)
- Understanding of Rails deployment, performance optimization, and security

**Core Responsibilities:**
- Technology strategy and product development roadmap
- Engineering team leadership and technical hiring (Rails + robotics)
- Platform architecture and scalability design (Rails + microservices)
- Safety systems and certification compliance
- IP strategy and technical partnerships

**Technical Involvement:** 60% hands-on, 40% management

### VP Engineering & Co-Founder: Operations-Focused Technical Leader
**Recommended Profile:**
- Strong in hardware/software integration and manufacturing
- Experience with service operations and field deployment
- Skills in supply chain, quality assurance, and technical operations

**Core Responsibilities:**
- Engineering operations and development processes
- Hardware procurement and fleet management
- Quality assurance and testing protocols
- Customer technical support and deployment
- Manufacturing partnerships and supply chain

**Technical Involvement:** 70% hands-on, 30% management

## Recommended Equity Distribution (Founding Team)

### Equal vs. Weighted Distribution Options

**Option 1: Equal Split (33/33/33)**
- Pros: Simple, avoids early conflicts, shows unity
- Cons: May not reflect different contributions over time


### Vesting Schedule Recommendation

- **4-year vesting with 1-year cliff** for all founders
- **Acceleration clauses** for termination without cause or company sale
- **Good/bad leaver provisions** to protect company if founder leaves

## Early-Stage Operating Model (Months 1-18)

### Decision-Making Structure
**Unanimous Decisions Required:**
- Equity dilution and major fundraising terms
- Key strategic pivots or major product changes
- Founder equity adjustments or new co-founder additions
- Board composition changes

**Majority Decisions (2 of 3):**
- Hiring decisions above senior engineer level
- Major technology architecture decisions
- Customer pricing and contract terms over €100k
- Office location and major operational changes

**Individual Authority:**
- CEO: External partnerships, investor communications, sales under €100k
- CTO: Technical hiring, development priorities, technology partnerships
- VP Engineering: Operations hiring, vendor relationships, quality processes

### Conflict Resolution Process
1. **Direct Discussion:** Founders discuss disagreements openly in weekly meetings
2. **Advisory Input:** Consult relevant advisor or board member for perspective
3. **External Mediation:** If needed, bring in neutral third-party for major decisions
4. **Documented Agreements:** All major decisions documented in board minutes

### Workload Distribution (Hours/Week)
**CEO Focus Areas (50 hours):**
- Customer development and sales: 40%
- Investor relations and fundraising: 25%
- Strategy and business development: 20%
- Technical oversight and product input: 15%

**CTO Focus Areas (55 hours):**
- Hands-on development and architecture: 60%
- Technical team management: 20%
- Technical partnerships and vendor relations: 15%
- Customer technical requirements: 5%

**VP Engineering Focus Areas (55 hours):**
- Hands-on engineering and operations: 70%
- Quality assurance and testing: 15%
- Manufacturing/procurement relationships: 10%
- Customer deployment and support: 5%

## Compensation Philosophy

### Equity Strategy
**Total Equity Pool:** 20% for employees (excluding founders)
- **Senior Leadership (C-level):** 1-3% equity
- **VP Level:** 0.5-1.5% equity
- **Senior Engineers/Managers:** 0.1-0.5% equity
- **Individual Contributors:** 0.01-0.2% equity

### Cash Compensation Strategy
**Market Positioning:** 90th percentile for critical roles, 75th percentile for others
**Geographic Adjustment:** Stockholm as base, +/- 15% for other locations
**Performance Bonuses:** 10-25% of base salary based on individual and company performance

### Benefits Package
- Comprehensive health insurance
- Generous parental leave (6 months)
- Professional development budget (€3k annually)
- Flexible working arrangements
- Stock option program with 4-year vesting

## Key Personnel Risks & Mitigation

### Founder Dependency Risk
**Mitigation Strategies:**
- Cross-training and knowledge documentation
- Strong second-tier leadership development
- Succession planning for key roles
- Key person insurance for founders

### Technical Talent Competition
**Mitigation Strategies:**
- Competitive compensation packages
- Meaningful equity participation
- Challenging technical problems and innovation focus
- Strong company culture and mission alignment
- Professional development and career growth paths

### Scaling Culture Risk
**Mitigation Strategies:**
- Early definition of company values and culture
- Structured onboarding and training programs
- Regular team building and company events
- Transparent communication and feedback systems
- Diversity and inclusion initiatives

## Talent Acquisition Strategy

### Recruitment Approach
**Technical Roles:** University partnerships, technical conferences, employee referrals
**Commercial Roles:** Industry networks, executive search firms, competitor talent
**International:** Local recruitment partners and in-country hiring

### Key Hiring Criteria
1. **Technical Excellence:** Proven track record in relevant domains
2. **Startup Mindset:** Comfort with ambiguity and rapid change
3. **Cultural Fit:** Alignment with company values and mission
4. **Growth Potential:** Ability to scale with the company
5. **European Focus:** Understanding of European markets and regulations

### Employee Development
**Career Pathing:** Clear advancement opportunities within each function
**Learning & Development:** €3k annual budget per employee for training
**Internal Mobility:** Opportunities to move between departments and roles
**Mentorship Program:** Pairing junior employees with senior leaders

## Performance Management

### Goal Setting Framework
**Company OKRs:** Quarterly objectives and key results cascaded company-wide
**Individual Goals:** Personal objectives aligned with company and department goals
**Performance Reviews:** Quarterly check-ins with annual comprehensive reviews

### Key Performance Indicators
**Engineering:** Code quality, feature delivery, system uptime
**Sales:** Revenue targets, customer acquisition, pipeline development
**Operations:** Service level achievement, customer satisfaction, efficiency metrics
**Leadership:** Team performance, strategic initiative delivery, cultural development

This organizational structure is designed to scale efficiently while maintaining the agility and innovation focus critical for success in the robotics and automation market.
